KORG DS-10 PLUS — decompiled

Both screens are drawn by a C reimplementation of the DS 2D graphics hardware, compiled to WebAssembly, reading the game's own asset files; the sound is the decompiled synth engine. A new song starts from the ROM's own blank template, and save writes a .dat the hardware would load. Tap the grid on 60_SYN_SEQ_PIANO to enter notes, drag the knobs on 61_SYN_EDIT, and touch a box on 90_MAP to move around. space starts and stops.
