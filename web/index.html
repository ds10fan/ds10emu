KORG DS-10 PLUS — decompiled

Both screens are drawn by a C reimplementation of the DS 2D graphics hardware, compiled to WebAssembly, reading the game's own asset files. Touch the keyboard to play it — that is the decompiled synth engine. Touch a box on 90_MAP to open that page, tap the grid on 60_SYN_SEQ_PIANO to sequence, and drag the knobs on 61_SYN_EDIT, 30_MIXER and 52_FX. space starts and stops; z and x shift the keyboard an octave.
